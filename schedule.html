On the Day Guidelines

  • Arrive at Greenwood High International School on time for smooth registration and credential distribution.
  • Every attendee receives a coloured wristband at registration. Wear it visibly for the entire fest.
  • Trophies, certificates, and prizes for every event are presented at the grand valedictory ceremony on Day 2.

Fest Timeline

Chronological schedule spanning morning inaugurations, midday arenas, and evening championship showdowns.

Day 1 • 08:30 AM - 09:45 AM

Opening Ceremony & Summit Cauldron Lighting

Inaugural keynote, lighting of the IGNITE cauldron, and formal introduction of participating school contingents, delegates, and judicial panels.

Day 1 • 10:00 AM - 01:30 PM

Morning Arenas: Business, Mystery & Sports Heats

Wolf of Wall Street opening bells & Bidding Wars, Veritas Room 1 investigations, Dyed in Crimson crime scene unlock, Football group ties, and Basketball qualifiers.

Day 1 • 02:30 PM - 06:30 PM

Afternoon Arena: Battle of the Bands & Stage Jams

Battle of the Bands Day 1 qualifiers, Pitch Perfect solo vocal knockouts, Respawn Minecraft minigame group clashes, Badminton ties, and Radioshack mini-game challenges.

Day 2 • 09:00 AM - 01:00 PM

Day 2 Breakthrough: Improvisation, Films & Semifinals

Director's Cut short film screenings, Kryptos Visos surprise 10-minute theme face painting, Respawn FIFA & Roblox finals, Table Tennis, Tennis, and Shooting eliminations.

Day 2 • 02:00 PM - 04:30 PM

Grand Finals: Runway Spectacular & Stadium Clashes

Dress to Impress sustainable runway showcases, Insync choreography crew finals, Wolf of Wall Street IPO final defense, and Football & Basketball Cup finals.

Day 2 • 05:00 PM - 06:30 PM

Valedictory & Awards Ceremony

Conferring of the IGNITE 2026 Overall Champions School Rolling Trophy, individual event gold medals, cash awards, and celebration of the fest.
