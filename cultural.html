Battle of the Bands

"For whom the band tolls. 12–15 bands. The ultimate showdown."

Battle of the Bands is a two-day interschool live music competition bringing together 12–15 student bands from across Bangalore.

Day 1 is a showcase round where every band plays a set to prove they belong on stage. The top bands advance to Day 2 for knockout head-to-head matches that decide who takes the title. Bring your best material, your tightest rhythms and your loudest sound.

What to bring & Rules
  • Bring your own instruments (guitars, keyboards, brass, pedals). Drum kit and piano are provided on stage.
  • Sound check time limits strictly enforced before performance.
  • Knockout format on Day 2 decides the champion band.

Dyed in Crimson

"One Murder, One Campus, Two days. Can you uncover what happened before the trail goes cold?"

Something has gone wrong on the Greenwood High campus, and the truth has been buried. Over two days, Dyed in Crimson turns the school into a living crime scene. Teams of investigators piece together evidence, track down leads, interrogate student suspects played by actors, crack ciphers and untangle a web of secrets and misdirection.

Around 150–200 players, in about 80 teams, compete across both days to see who can solve the mystery before time runs out. The clock is ticking, the suspect is watching, and every detail matters.

What to bring & Rules
  • Carry notebooks, pens, and analytical wits.
  • Respect actor boundaries and stay within active crime scene perimeters.
  • Strictly no collusion between independent detective units.
Fee

Wolf of Wall Street

"Trade the market. Survive the chaos. Become the Wolf."

Step into the fast-paced world of Wall Street, where fortunes can rise in seconds and one decision can change everything. In this two-day immersive business and trading simulation, teams take control of a struggling company, build their strategy, and enter a live fictional stock market where they trade, negotiate, compete, take risks and chase the biggest fortune.

From “Sell Me This Pen” and high-stakes Bidding Wars to confidential Insider Information and market-shaking Breaking News, every round puts your strategy to the test. The competition reaches its climax with the market's final shock, the closing bell, and a high-pressure IPO-style pitch where teams defend their company before the judges.

What to bring & Rules
  • Bring a charged laptop with spreadsheet capability.
  • Review the case study and background info package sent prior to the fest.
  • Unannounced flash crashes and breaking announcements occur in real time.

Veritas

"Can you crack the case before it's too late?"

Veritas is an interactive escape-room-style investigation designed to test critical thinking, teamwork, observation, and problem-solving skills.

Presented with a case, teams investigate the room in groups of 3–6 detectives to uncover hidden clues, solve puzzles, crack codes, and piece together the evidence, making a final accusation before the one hour of time is up. Winners are decided based on correct suspects, motives, and hints used. Each day has a different winner!

What to bring & Rules
  • Strict 1-hour escape room time countdown.
  • No external electronic communication allowed inside investigation rooms.
  • Points scored for correct suspects, accurate motive, and minimal hint usage.

Director's Cut

"Create the story, adapt to the challenge, and own the final cut."

Step into the director’s chair, where every story is yours to shape. Day 1 tests your dramatic instincts: perform a prepared piece from a creative brief, then take on an unexpected, unscripted improvisation challenge.

On Day 2, bring your ideas to the screen. Conceive, shoot, and edit an original 3–7 minute short film that responds to a surprise prompt or constraint. From script to final screening, show us you can adapt, collaborate, and own the story.

What to bring & Rules
  • Bring all your own filming, audio recording, and video editing equipment.
  • Day 1: Stage acting & live improv; Day 2: 3–7 min short film screened before judges.
  • All footage must be captured on campus during the allocated fest time window.

Dress to Impress

"Think beyond the theme. Where creativity meets competition."

Teams of 4–6 take the stage for a 7-minute runway presentation followed by a short explanation of their designs.

Round 1 (Reforged): Craft outfits inspired by mythological houses, using at least 40% upcycled materials to bring ancient tales into contemporary fashion.
Round 2 (Legacy, Reworn): Reimagine traditional Indian attire across historical eras, balancing tradition and fresh interpretation.

What to bring & Rules
  • Prepare and bring all clothing, fabrics, makeup, and accessories from home.
  • Round 1 requires minimum 40% certified upcycled / repurposed material.
  • 7-minute runway showcase followed by judges Q&A on concept and design process.
