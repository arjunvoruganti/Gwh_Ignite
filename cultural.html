Cultural Events

Ten premier cultural competitions testing stage charisma, intellect, mystery deduction, and artistic flair.

👥 3 to 5 people per team

Wolf of Wall Street

"Trade the market. Survive the chaos. Become the Wolf."

Step into the fast-paced world of Wall Street, where fortunes can rise in seconds and one decision can change everything. In this two-day immersive business and trading simulation, teams take control of a struggling company, build their strategy, and enter a live fictional stock market where they trade, negotiate, compete, take risks and chase the biggest fortune.

From “Sell Me This Pen” and high-stakes Bidding Wars to confidential Insider Information and market-shaking Breaking News, every round puts your strategy to the test. As the market shifts and unexpected events unfold, teams must decide when to play it safe, when to take the risk, and when to make their move. The competition reaches its climax with the market's final shock, the closing bell, and a high-pressure IPO-style pitch where teams defend their company, strategy and valuation before the judges.

Additional Details & Rules Participants are also required to go through the case study and the background information provided prior to the event.
Format: 2-Day Live Market Simulation Register Team →
👥 3 to 6 people per team

Veritas

"Can you crack the case before it's too late?"

Veritas is an interactive escape-room-style investigation designed to test critical thinking, teamwork, observation, and problem-solving skills. Presented with a case, teams will investigate the room in groups of 3–6 detectives to uncover hidden clues, solve puzzles, crack codes, and piece together the evidence, making a final accusation before the one hour of time is up.

Winners will be decided based on correct suspects, motives, and hints used. Each day will have a different winner.

Additional Details & Rules 1-hour time limit. No external mobile communication allowed inside investigation rooms.
Format: Interactive Escape Room Register Team →
👤 Solo event (no teams)

Through the Lens

"Frame it. Feel it. Tell it."

Photography: Three rounds. Three themes. One winner. From raw emotions to high-speed sports to the rhythm of music, each round challenges you to capture something new. Show us how you see the world, frame by frame, and prove you've got the eye to win.

Additional Details & Rules Participants must use DSLR or mirrorless cameras. Smartphone photography is not permitted.
Format: 3 Themed Photo Rounds Register Solo →
🎮 Solo (Roblox/FIFA) & 4 per team (Minecraft)

Respawn

"Rise. Fall. Respawn."

Respawn is the ultimate gaming showdown. Battle in teams of 4 through action-packed Minecraft minigames. Win the most mini-games in a match to earn 3 points, top the group, and advance to the knockout stage.

Individuals battle it out on the pitch with Champions League Format, with random clubs given for each match and knockouts leading to the final.

The Roblox segment will be a two-day challenge with an Obby race on Day 1 and Dress to Impress on Day 2. Players will have to adapt quickly to different games, teams, and surprise challenges. The best players and teams will make it to the final rounds and compete for the win.

Additional Details & Rules Participants must bring their own laptops for Minecraft and devices for Roblox (including chargers).
Games: Minecraft, FIFA, Roblox Register Arena →
👤 Solo event (no teams)

Pitch Perfect

"Every voice gets a chance. Every note is a statement. Step onto the stage and make it yours."

Soloists will battle it out to steal the spotlight by showing off their vocal abilities, dominating the stage, and proving that one voice is all it takes. Every note is a chance to stand out, captivate the audience, and claim the crown as the most impressive voice.

Round one is your chance to stand out, and the final round is where legends are made. So take a look around and own the stage.

Additional Details & Rules Solo vocalists. Backing tracks or acoustic accompaniment allowed. Time limit strictly enforced.
Format: 2 Knockout Rounds Register Solo →
👥 2 people per team

Kryptos Visos

"Bring your brushes, beat the clock, and let your creativity IGNITE!"

A face painting competition where teams of two, one model and one painter, take on a themed challenge and must create a face painting artwork based on it.

On Day 1, the theme is announced ahead of time, giving teams a chance to prepare. Teams that advance to the second round face a new twist, where the Day 2 theme is revealed just 10 minutes before painting begins and each duo gets a different theme, testing how well they can think and create on the spot.

Additional Details & Rules Participants must bring their own skin-safe painting materials and clean-up supplies.
Team: 1 Painter + 1 Model Register Duo →
👥 4 to 5 people per team

Directors Cut

"Create the story, adapt to the challenge, and own the final cut."

Step into the director’s chair, where every story can change in an instant. Teams of 4–5 will be given a creative brief and challenged to bring their own characters, plot, and vision to life through performance.

After their main performance on Day 1, teams will be given an unexpected scenario and challenged to improvise a brand-new scene with no preparation. Then, on Day 2, teams will face a fresh creative challenge: transforming a given genre and set of constraints into an original 3–7 minute short film. From the first line of the script to the final improvised scene, every moment is yours to shape.

Additional Details & Rules Day 1: Character performance & live improvisation. Day 2: 3–7 min original short film production.
Format: Theater & Short Film Register Team →
👥 1 to 9 people per team

Insync

"Feel the beat. Move In Sync."

Take the stage at In Sync - An electrifying celebration of dance, expression, and creativity, bringing together dancers to experience the power of movement. From dance battles of creativity to timed choreography challenges, watch dancers push their limits, embrace the unexpected, and ignite the stage.

More than just a performance, InSync is a space where dancers can break free from boundaries and transform emotions and ideas into art.

Additional Details & Rules Open to soloists up to 9-member crews. Audio tracks must be submitted during morning briefing.
Format: Choreography & Battles Register Crew →
👥 3 to 8 people per team

Battle of the Bands

"For whom the band tolls. 12- 15 bands. The ultimate showdown."

Bring your sound to Battle of the Bands, a two-day interschool live music competition that brings together student bands from across different schools in Bangalore.

Each performance is an electric display of courage, teamwork, and musicianship, get ready to compete with the city's best musicians and to be amongst the final champions of the showdown. 'Cause where words fail, music speaks.

Additional Details & Rules Stage backline and drum kit provided. Bands must bring their own instruments, pedals, and accessories.
Slots: 12-15 Interschool Bands Register Band →
👥 3 people per team

Dyed in Crimson

"One Murder, One Campus, Two days. Can you uncover what happened before the trail goes cold?"

Something has gone wrong at Greenwood High, and the truth is hiding in plain sight. For two days, the whole campus becomes a live crime scene, and you are the detective. Watch the story unfold in live theatre, question people who all seem to be hiding something, and follow clues to corners of the school you thought you knew. Nobody is who they seem, and every answer raises a new question.

Gather your team of up to three and get ready to crack ciphers, solve riddles, test your memory, and sharpen your eyes. Surprises can arrive at any moment, so stay alert, because the case may change when you least expect it. This is far more than a treasure hunt. It rewards sharp thinking, teamwork, and the courage to change your mind. Come ready to look closer, because the case is open and we need detectives.

Additional Details & Rules Campus-wide investigation over 2 days. Teams of exactly 3 detectives.
Format: 2-Day Live Campus Mystery Register Detectives →
👥 4 to 6 people per team

Dress to Impress

"Think beyond the theme — Where creativity meets competition."

Fabric, myth, and tradition collide on the IGNITE runway. Dress to Impress challenges teams to reimagine ancient lore and iconic Indian eras through cutting-edge, sustainable fashion.

From breathing new life into repurposed materials to restyling legendary heritage looks, this is your chance to showcase your design process, captivate the spotlight, and prove that true style knows no boundaries.

Additional Details & Rules Sustainable and repurposed materials requirement. Concept briefing submitted prior to runway showcase.
