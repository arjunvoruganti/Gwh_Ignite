Battle of the Bands

Battle of the Bands is a two-day interschool live music competition that brings together student bands from across schools in Bangalore. Featuring 12-15 participating bands and a showcase round on Day 1, and a knockout - style competition on Day 2, the competition will unfold over the two days into a melodious matchup.

What to bring & Rules
  • Bring your own instruments (guitars, keyboards, brass, pedals). Drum kit and piano are provided on stage.
  • Sound check time limits strictly enforced before performance.
  • Knockout format on Day 2 decides the champion band.

Veritas

Veritas is an interactive escape-room-style investigation designed to test critical thinking, teamwork, observation, and problem-solving skills. Presented with a case, teams will investigate the room in groups of 3–6 detectives to uncover hidden clues, solve puzzles, crack codes, and piece together the evidence, making a final accusation before the one hour of time is up. Winners will be decided based on correct suspects, motives, and hints used. Each day will have a different winner.

What to bring & Rules
  • Strict 1-hour escape room time countdown.
  • No external electronic communication allowed inside investigation rooms.
  • Points scored for correct suspects, accurate motive, and minimal hint usage.
Fee
₹600 per team

Dress to Impress

Teams of 4–6 will take the stage for a 7-minute runway performance showcasing their interpretation of assigned themes, followed by a brief design explanation. Round 1 (Reforged) requires at least 40% upcycled materials based on mythological houses. Round 2 (Legacy, Reworn) asks finalists to restyle traditional Indian attire through historical eras.

Kyrptos Visos

A face painting competition where teams of two, one model and one painter, take on a themed challenge and must create a face painting artwork based on it. On day 1, the theme is announced ahead of time, giving teams a chance to prepare. Teams that advance to the second round face a new twist, where the day 2 theme is revealed just 10 minutes before painting begins, and each team gets a different theme, testing how well they can think and create on the spot.

What to bring & Rules
  • Bring your own skin-safe face paints, palettes, and brushes.
  • Each team consists of exactly 1 painter and 1 model.
  • Judged on creative interpretation, technique, detail, and theme adherence.
