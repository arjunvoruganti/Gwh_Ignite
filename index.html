A Welcome From The Team

A message from the student leadership leading IGNITE 2026.

Student Leadership Directorate

The IGNITE Heads

Dear Participants,

It is with great pride and excitement that we welcome you to IGNITE 2026, on the 13th and 14th of November. Across two action-packed days, our campus will host 12 cultural events and 8 sports tournaments, uniting creativity, talent, and sportsmanship under one unforgettable celebration.

IGNITE is not just another fest. It is Bangalore's biggest student-run festival, and what makes it truly special is that it stands for something larger than itself. Every moment of celebration, every competition, and every memory created helps us raise funds for the education of underprivileged girls, a cause that fuels our passion to make IGNITE bigger and more impactful every year.

At the heart of IGNITE is our beloved mascot, the Phoenix, a timeless symbol of resilience, rebirth, and boundless energy. Just like the phoenix, IGNITE rises each year, brighter and stronger, to inspire everyone who becomes part of it.

Warm regards,
The IGNITE Heads

Before You Come

Essential highlights every school contingent and visitor should keep in mind.

All fest rules →

Registration

Open 2–10 November. Cultural events are registered and paid online. Sports are registered online and paid at the counter.

Entry

Wear your regular school uniform and bring your 2026–27 school ID. You will receive an official wristband to wear all day.

Food

No outside food or drinks. Gourmet food trucks and stalls on campus operate with coupons sold at venue counters.

Prizes

Every event features winner trophies and certificates, awarded at the grand Day 2 valedictory ceremony.
