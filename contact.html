How to Register

Cultural events: Register and pay online.
Sports: Register online, then pay at the counter when you arrive on campus. You will receive an official receipt.

Registrations run from 2 to 10 November. Each school can enter one team per sport. Individual event limits, fees, and rules are detailed on the Cultural and Sports pages.
